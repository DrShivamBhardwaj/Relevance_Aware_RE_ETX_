## Manuscript-safe claims

1. Intel: proposed has substantially lower utility-target JS than resource-adaptive, but resource-adaptive has lower RMSE, traffic, total modeled energy, and maximum relay energy.
2. Intel: against FedCG-adapted, RMSE and systems-cost differences are not significant after Holm correction.
3. HAR: proposed improves held-out accuracy and Macro-F1 over resource-adaptive and FedCG-adapted, but uses more traffic, energy, and maximum relay energy.
4. Utility-target JS is endogenous to the controller. Independent temperature/class coverage is reported separately and must not be described as proving population representativeness.
5. Adaptive update fidelity accounts for most of the communication reduction versus the same proposed selector at fixed 50% Top-k.
6. ns-3 replay validates communication consequences of the offered traffic profile; it does not execute the Python HFL controller.
7. Reported joules are modeled local-training plus model-update transport energy, not measured device lifetime or sustainability.
